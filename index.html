About ALLID

Mushrooms are sensitive to their environment. Small changes in humidity, temperature and fresh air can make a big difference.

ALLID is at the idea stage. We are exploring how automation could help growers, and we would like to talk to people who produce mushrooms, or who want to build this with us.
